Warranty and Defective Goods Policy

Items found to be defective are covered by a 12-month warranty from the
purchase date. Defect claims within 365 days are eligible for a refund.

Defect claims made more than 365 days after purchase are denied.

Unlike damaged goods, a defect claim does NOT require a photograph.

Defective-item refunds above the escalation threshold must be escalated to a
manager.
