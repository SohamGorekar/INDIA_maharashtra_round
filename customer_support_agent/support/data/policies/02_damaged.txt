Damaged Goods Policy

Items that arrive damaged are eligible for a refund within 45 days of the
purchase date.

A PHOTOGRAPH OF THE DAMAGE IS REQUIRED before any refund can be issued. If
the customer has not supplied a photo, the agent must request one and must
not issue a refund in the same conversation.

Damage claims made more than 45 days after purchase are denied.

Damaged-item refunds above the escalation threshold must be escalated to a
manager, exactly like any other high-value refund.
