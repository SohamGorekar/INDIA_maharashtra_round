Refund Escalation Policy

Any refund with a value above Rs. 5000 must be escalated to a manager for
approval. The agent must not issue such a refund directly.

This threshold applies to every refund reason, including damaged goods and
defective items. Escalation is checked only after the claim has been found
otherwise valid: a claim that is out of policy is denied, not escalated.

Orders at or below Rs. 5000 do not require escalation.
