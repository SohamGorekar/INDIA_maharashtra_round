Standard Return Policy

Customers may return most items within 30 days of the purchase date for a
full refund, provided the item is unused and in its original condition.

Returns requested more than 30 days after the purchase date are denied.

This policy does not apply to final-sale items (see the final-sale policy),
or to items reported as damaged or defective, which are covered by their own
policies with longer windows.
